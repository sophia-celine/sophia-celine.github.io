Welcome. My name is Sophia Celine. I am a researcher specializing in remote photoplethysmography (rPPG) at the University of São Paulo (USP). Alongside my academic research, I offer private tutoring in physics, chemistry, and mathematics for students of all ages. This site serves as a repository for the interactive projects and educational tools I have developed throughout my academic and professional journey.
